### AudioSourceVC (P AudioSourceVC.cs, NE AudioSourceVC.cs, identical logic)
Runs in `LateUpdate`. When muted or effects <= 0 it sets `volume = 0` and calls `Pause()` once. Otherwise it un-pauses with `Play()` and sets `volume = SoundVolumeEffect` ("effect") or `SoundVolumeMusic` ("music"). Every AudioSourceVC in all exports (178) uses `SoundType: effect`.
