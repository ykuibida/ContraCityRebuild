# Audit: battle flow without weapons (GameFlow / BattleUI / PlayerVitals / BattleRoom / MapList)

Abbreviations: **P** = Penta 2016 (`.../Assembly-CSharp/`), **NE** = New Era (`NE_Scripts_Decrypted/`), **ours** = `W:/ContraCityRebuild/Assets/...`.
NE classes: `喬` = MyRoomSetting, `嗱` = GameScore, `啺` = ScoreTeam, `啼` = ScorePlayer, `嗉` = MapList, `喡` = OfflineBattleStats.
"NE-add" marks code that is clearly the rebuilders' own addition (HTTP battle server, `[SPAWN]` / `[OFFLINE]` logs, Russian strings, "Normalize...").

## Top list: the most important differences

1. **What happens after the round.** In P (`PlayerManager.cs:2810` NewGame → `GameHUD.cs:2708` SetTimeOverReadyState) and on the NE server path, the player goes to **TimeOverReady**. The HUD shows "PRESS FIRE TO RESPAWN" and fire respawns the player **in the same team** (`GameHUD.cs:2570`, NE `GameHUD.cs:3845`). NE offline (`OfflineMatchController.cs:703-726`) auto-respawns after **8 s** with no team choice. Ours (`BattleUI.cs:123` → `GameFlow.RestartRound` + `InitSpawn`) resets the team to -1 and **opens the team selection again** (in DM it runs the 7 s countdown again). That matches neither original.
2. **Round end conditions are incomplete.** Ours ends a round only on the timer, and only in non-CTF/CP modes (`BattleUI.cs:121-122`). CTF/CP **never end**. There is **no frag limit**. NE offline also ends on `Frags`: in DM when one player's Kill >= Frags, in TDM when a team's Kill >= Frags (`OfflineMatchController.cs:175-179, 198-229`; the default Frags for DM/TDM is 100 through `SelectedFragIndex`). NE also increments team **Wins** (`:678-686`, shown in the results). P does all of this on the server (TimeOver event, `PlayerManager.cs:2767`, Wins at `:2778-2783`).
3. **At time over the originals kill everyone.** P `PlayerManager.cs:2786-2804` and NE `PlayerManager.cs:8170-8216` call KillEnemy on all players and `KillMe(true)` on the local one. Ours leaves the fighter alive (input is only blocked) until RestartRound destroys it.
4. **No death camera or spectating.** Originals (`P PlayerManager.cs:2532-2608`, NE `:7382`, `:8000`): on death the TPS camera sits 10 units behind, looking at the killer, and after **2 s** switches to the killer's TPS camera. RMB in Dead/DeadReady calls `nextCamera()` to cycle team-mates (P `GameHUD.cs:2536-2539`, NE `GameHUD.cs:3819-3822`). After a team is joined, NE switches to that team's camera (`SwitchToSelectedTeamCamera`, `GameHUD.cs:2099`). Ours has none of this, although our tips advertise "RMB – Change camera". The death animation (`ActorAnimator.Defeat`, P `:2600`) is also missing.
5. **The respawn countdown digit is off by one.** Originals compute `(timeOfDeath + delay*1000 - now)/1000` with **integer truncation**, so the display runs 6…1, then **0**, and then the player becomes Ready (P `GameHUD.cs:2164`, NE `:3470`). Ours uses `Ceil`, so it shows 7…1 and never 0 (`BattleUI.cs:205`). The delay value itself: **7 s** matches NE (`GameHUD.cs:30`, `:698`, premium ignored). P uses **11 s**, or **6 s** for premium (`GameHUD.cs:189`, `:413-419`).
6. **Team switch from the Esc menu.** This exists only in NE, and it is NE-add (`TryGetMenuTeamSwitchState` `GameHUD.cs:2439`, server `TryRequestTeamSwitch` `BattleServerHttpClient.cs:3177`). NE sends a **spawn request into the other team**: an immediate respawn, with no death and no countdown. Ours (`BattleUI.cs:686-691`) despawns the fighter and starts the **7 s respawn countdown**. Our **90 s cooldown** is a guess (the comment says so). In NE the cooldown comes from the server (`teamSwitchAllowedAtUnixMilliseconds`, `:6615`), and the value is not in the client. Ours also starts the cooldown at the **first** team join (`BattleUI.cs:55`). P has no team switch at all.
7. **Pickup amounts and respawn times.**
   - **Amounts.** NE restores by size, not by `Value`: a small kit (`SubType==1` or `Value==50`) gives **+ceil(max/2)**, a big kit gives a **full refill** (`PlayerManager.cs:4636-4649`). Health max = max(100, battle max health). Armor max is fixed at **100** (`:4593-4634`). Ours adds the raw `Value` (+100 / +50), so the result differs as soon as MaxHealth > 100.
   - **Respawn time.** Ours uses each marker's `RespawnDelay` from the scene data (Value 100 → 20000 ms, Value 50 → 10000 ms; P default 15000, `ItemMapXML.cs:9`). NE uses a fixed **7 s** (`PlayerManager.cs:4588`; NE `ItemMapXML.cs:9` default 7000).
   - **Ammo.** Ours always consumes ammo kits (invented placeholder). NE consumes one only if `CanAddAmmoToReserve` allows it.
   - P's client adds the server amount without any cap (`PlayerManager.cs:1468-1500`). Our comment claims "health capped 100 from Penta LocalPlayerManager.PickItem". That is wrong: that tutorial code adds the *Health* item to **Energy (armor)**, capped at 100 (`LocalPlayerManager.cs:1364-1386`).
8. **Death out of the map is invented.** `PlayerVitals.KillY = lowest spawn − 300` (`GameFlow.cs:106`, `PlayerVitals.cs:111-115`) exists in neither client. P has no client check. NE has a `PitKillZone` stub that never fires (`CharacterMotor.cs:756` returns false) plus NE-add under-map **recovery**: a teleport back to the last safe ground within 8 m (`CharacterMotor.cs:112-134`, `:465-480`).
9. **Spawn point choice.**
   - **DM.** Ours uses **every point except Respawn_T0** (`GameFlow.cs:136`). NE DM uses the **neutral Respawn_T0** points first, and falls back to T0/T1/T2 only if there are fewer than 2 (`MainNetworkController.cs:990`, `:1451-1484`). This is the opposite of ours.
   - **Spawn transform.** NE spawns at the child `RespawnPoint` of the `Respawn_Tn` root, snapped to the ground (`:1500-1550`, `:1928-1944`). Ours uses the root position + 0.5 up (the +0.5 is invented).
   - **Selection.** Ours picks uniformly at random. NE uses a no-repeat "bag" (NE-add, `:1424-1449`). In P the server chooses the position (`PlayerManager.cs:1771`).
10. **Base gates are not ported.** P `PlayerManager.SetupGates(team)` (`:1073`, called from SpawnMe) and NE (`:3856`) make the enemy base gates (`finish4/GatesRed*`, `GatesBlue*`, plus triggers) solid for one team and passable for the other. Our scenes contain 24 GatesRed and 24 GatesBlue objects, and no code handles them.
11. **Played-time and stats.**
    - **Ours:** seconds of unscaled time while `State.Battle`, including team selection and the results screen, flushed only on exit or quit (`GameFlow.cs:77-86`). Kills are never recorded: `GameFlow.Kills` is never incremented and no "kill" stat is written.
    - **NE:** per **round**, minutes = `ceil(elapsed/60000)`, at least 1, capped at TimeLimit+1, counted from round StartTime. Committed at round end and on exit, with kills, deaths, wins and losses per mode and per map (`喡.cs:100-140`, `:416-443`, `:466-530`).
    - **P:** server-side.
12. **Exit destination.** NE exit returns to **Fight → RoomList** (`GameHUD.cs:4392`, `NetworkManager.cs:682`) after committing stats. Ours loads `_Lobby` with no tab choice (`GameFlow.cs:88-96`). Verify which tab the lobby opens on.
13. **Map list data is reconstructed.** Players "4…14" with default index 3, and base modes 15 vs 3 for some maps (`MapList.cs:235`, `:264-270`), are guesses. The comment admits this. NE's defaults when the server list omits fields: players **"2,4,6,8,10,12,14"**, modes **3** (DM+TDM), dp **0** (`嗉.cs:277-297`). NE also adds the map `Arena_3lvl_sniper` (`嗉.cs:323-351`).
